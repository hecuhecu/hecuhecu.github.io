## Learn hand lettering with AI feedback — on real paper

Calli AI is a structured hand-lettering course that works the way real lettering practice does: **on paper, with a real pen**. Print a guide sheet, practice a lesson, snap a photo — and get instant AI feedback that shows you exactly what to fix.

## How it works

1. **Print a guide sheet** — every lesson comes with a printable practice sheet with baseline and x-height guides
2. **Practice with your favorite pen** — brush pens (like Tombow Fudenosuke) or monoline pens both work; the app detects your pen type automatically
3. **Snap a photo** — the app straightens your photo automatically
4. **Get AI feedback** — scores and coaching comments on 4 axes: stroke-width contrast, baseline consistency, slant, and spacing
5. **Level up** — pass lessons to progress through a structured curriculum: basic strokes → lowercase → beyond

## Features

- **Structured curriculum**: learn in the right order, one skill at a time, with pass/fail lesson grading
- **AI red-pen feedback**: photo-based scoring with specific, actionable comments and a daily drill suggestion
- **Before & after**: auto-generated Day 1 vs. today comparison images that show your real progress
- **Progress tracking**: score trends across all submissions
- **No account needed**: your practice records stay on your device

## Pricing

Calli AI is subscription-based: **Yearly US$39.99/year with a 3-day free trial**, or Monthly US$12.99/month. That's less than a single in-person lettering workshop.
