## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a subscription to listen?

No. You can listen to your first story all the way through for free.

Premium (subscription) adds a new story every day, adding more wishes, all scenes, and more. The annual plan includes a 7-day free trial.

### Where is what I write sent?

To create your story and its audio, the wish text, category, your name, your partner's name, job, and similar details you enter are sent to Google's Gemini (Firebase AI Logic). What you send is not used to train AI.

The generated stories and audio, your wish list, and your records are stored on your device. See the [Privacy Policy](/apps/manifest-story/privacy_en.html) for details.

### Will my wish come true?

The App is for imagery practice and relaxation before sleep. It does not guarantee that any wish will come true, and it is not intended as medical care or psychological treatment. See the [Terms of Use](/apps/manifest-story/terms_en.html) for details.

### I see a message that I have reached today's limit

There is a daily limit on how many stories can be generated, which resets when the date changes. Stories that have already been generated can be played as many times as you like.

### My story will not generate

Please check your connection. Generating a story requires an internet connection. Try again after a while, and if it still does not work, contact us at the email address above. Stories already generated can be played offline.

### I want playback to continue when the screen is off

Playback continues when you close the screen or lock your device. To stop it automatically before you fall asleep, use the sleep timer.

### I am not getting reminders

In your device's Settings, check that notifications are allowed for Solia. Reminders are scheduled on your device, and you can change the time in the App's settings.

### How do I cancel the free trial?

- iOS: Settings > your name > Subscriptions
- Android: Google Play Store app > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions

**If you do not cancel before the free period ends (on iOS, at least 24 hours before), the annual plan fee is charged when the period ends.**

After cancelling, you can keep using Premium until the free period or the current billing period ends.

### My stories disappeared after I changed phones

Solia has no accounts, and your wishes, stories, and records are stored only on your device, so they are not carried over to a new device.

If you have already purchased, you can restore your subscription using the same Apple or Google account from "Restore purchases" on the purchase screen.

### I want a refund

Refunds follow each store's policy, and we cannot issue refunds directly. If you subscribed through the App Store, please use Apple's support page ([https://reportaproblem.apple.com/](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/)); if through Google Play, use Google Play Help ([https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637)).

### I want to delete my data

You can delete a wish at any time from the wish list. Deleting the App removes all wishes, stories, audio, and records on your device.

If you have a paid plan, you must cancel the subscription separately from deleting your data.
