# Privacy Policy

hecuhecu (the "Provider") sets out this Privacy Policy (this "Policy") regarding the handling of Users' personal information in the application "Solia" (the "App"), which the Provider provides.

## Section 1 Personal Information

"Personal information" means personal information as defined in Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information: information about a living individual that can identify that specific individual by the name, date of birth, or other description contained in it.

## Section 2 Information We Collect

### (1) Account information

The App has no account registration, and no name or email address is required.

### (2) Information stored on the device

The following information is stored only in the database and files on your device, and the Provider does not collect it (except where it is sent for story generation as described in Section 6).

- Profile information such as your wishes, your name, your partner's name, the names of people close to you who may appear in stories and their relationship to you, and job
- The text of generated stories and their audio
- Your wish list, your "came true" records, and the number of nights you have continued
- Settings such as the chosen scene, voice, sleep timer, and reminders

### (3) Information sent externally to generate stories

To create a story and its audio, the following is sent to an external AI service (see Section 6).

- The wish text you enter, the wish category, your name, your partner's name, the names of people close to you and their relationship to you, your job, and any additional details you enter
- The display language and the type of voice you selected
- The text of the generated story (to create the audio)

Wish text, names, and similar input may include personal information or content about your mind and body, relationships, family, work, or money. The background images used for stories are bundled with the App, and no images are sent externally or generated by AI.

### (4) Device and usage information

- Device type, OS version, App version
- How the App is used (screens viewed, stories generated and played, purchase-screen views, and the like). This does not include the wishes, names, or story content that the User entered
- An anonymous identifier randomly issued for each device (a RevenueCat anonymous ID), used to link usage with purchase information

The App does not show ads, does not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA, Advertising ID), and does not perform tracking through App Tracking Transparency.

### (5) Purchase information

- Subscription status, and the history of purchases, renewals, and cancellations
- Receipt information issued by Apple or Google

**Payment details such as credit card numbers are managed by Apple or Google; the Provider does not collect them.**

### (6) Inquiry information

The email address, the content of the inquiry, and device information that the User provides when making an inquiry.

### (7) Access to device features

- **Notifications**: used to show nightly reminders. These are local notifications scheduled on the device, not delivered from a server
- **Audio playback**: background playback is used so that a story keeps playing when you close the screen or lock your device
- **Network**: used to generate stories. Stories that have already been generated can be played offline

The App does not access the microphone, camera, photo library, location, or contacts.

## Section 3 Purposes of Use

The Provider uses the information it collects for the following purposes.

1. Providing, maintaining, protecting, and improving the App
2. Generating stories and their audio
3. Improving the App based on analysis of how it is used
4. Providing and managing subscriptions
5. Responding to inquiries from Users
6. Delivering notices about the App
7. Purposes incidental to the purposes above

## Section 4 Provision to Third Parties

The Provider does not provide personal information to third parties without obtaining the User's prior consent, except in the following cases.

1. Where required by law
2. Where it is necessary to protect the life, body, or property of a person and it is difficult to obtain the User's consent
3. Where it is particularly necessary to improve public health or promote the sound growth of children, and it is difficult to obtain the User's consent
4. Where it is necessary to cooperate with a national agency, a local government, or a party entrusted by either, in carrying out duties prescribed by law, and obtaining the User's consent would be likely to impede the performance of those duties
5. Where provision accompanies the outsourcing or use of external services set out in Section 5

## Section 6 Story Generation and the Use of AI

1. The wish text, wish category, your name, your partner's name, the names of people close to you and their relationship to you, job, additional details, display language, and the type of voice you selected are sent to Google's Gemini (Firebase AI Logic) in order to generate the story text. The text of the generated story is then sent to Gemini again in order to generate the audio.
2. No identifying information such as an email address is attached when data is sent. However, whatever the User types into fields such as their name or their partner's name is sent as entered.
3. The App uses the Gemini API under a paid plan, and the content sent is not used by Google to train AI models.
4. Users should take care not to include sensitive personal information, confidential information, or content that could harm real third parties, of their own or of others, in the wish text or any input field. Entering a partner's name, the names of people close to you, or a job is optional.
5. Apart from the features described above, the App does not send User input to any external AI service. The App also does not send wishes, names, or story content to usage analytics (Firebase Analytics).
6. Generated stories are works of fiction created by AI, and their accuracy and appropriateness are not guaranteed.

## Section 7 Retention Period

1. Wishes, stories, audio, and records stored on the device are kept until the User deletes them or deletes the App. Deleting the App also removes the data on the device. To save device storage, the App may automatically delete the audio of older stories; the text of those stories remains.
2. You can delete a wish at any time from the wish list.
3. The Provider does not store the content sent to the external AI service. How it is handled on the external service's side follows that service provider's policy.
4. The Provider retains any other personal information only for the period necessary to achieve the purposes of use.

## Section 8 Security Measures

The Provider takes necessary and appropriate measures, such as encrypting communications and restricting access permissions, to prevent leakage, loss, or damage of personal information and otherwise to manage it securely.

## Section 9 Users' Rights

1. A User may request disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, suspension of use, or suspension of provision to third parties, of their own personal information held by the Provider.
2. Requests under the preceding item should be sent to the contact point in Section 12. The Provider will verify the requester's identity and respond in accordance with applicable law.
3. Data on the device can be deleted through operations in the App or by deleting the App.

## Section 10 Use by Minors

A minor who uses the App must obtain the prior consent of a parent or other legal representative.

## Section 11 Changes to This Policy

1. Except for matters prescribed by law or otherwise stated in this Policy, this Policy may be changed without notice to Users.
2. Where a change is material, the Provider will announce the content of the change and the date it takes effect, by display within the App or by other appropriate means.
