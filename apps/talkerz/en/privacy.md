# Privacy Policy

hecuhecu (the "Provider") sets out this Privacy Policy (this "Policy") regarding the handling of Users' personal information in the application "Talkerz" (the "App"), which the Provider provides.

## Section 1 Personal Information

"Personal information" means personal information as defined in Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information: information about a living individual that can identify that specific individual by the name, date of birth, or other description contained in it.

## Section 2 Information We Collect

### (1) Account information

The App has no account registration, and no name or email address is required.

### (2) Information stored on the device

The following information is stored only in the database and files on your device, and the Provider does not collect it (except where it is sent for the use of AI described in Section 6).

- Practice records (scores, feedback, transcripts, consecutive days, experience points earned)
- Recorded audio
- Scripts you load (text, PDF, photos) and the results of reading them aloud
- Answers given during onboarding (concerns, frequency, situations, age group, practice time, and the like)
- Settings such as reminders

### (3) Recorded audio, transcripts, and input

The audio and transcripts recorded in practice, the industry and job entered in mock interviews, and the topic text used to generate outline hints are sent to an external AI service in order to generate scoring, transcription, and advice (see Section 6). Scripts and photos you load for script reading are not sent.

### (4) Device and usage information

- Device type, OS version, App version
- How the App is used (screens viewed, practices started and completed, scores, purchase-screen views, and the like). This does not include what the User said, recordings, transcripts, or scripts
- An anonymous identifier randomly issued for each device (a RevenueCat anonymous ID), used to link usage with purchase information
- Crash information (release versions only)
- Advertising identifier (IDFA). This may be collected for ad delivery and measurement when the App is used free of charge (see Section 7)

### (5) Purchase information

- Subscription status, and the history of purchases, renewals, and cancellations
- Receipt information issued by Apple

**Payment details such as credit card numbers are managed by Apple; the Provider does not collect them.**

### (6) Inquiry information

The email address, the content of the inquiry, and device information that the User provides when making an inquiry.

### (7) Access to device features

- **Microphone**: used to record speaking practice
- **Camera and photo library**: used to capture or select a script to read aloud in practice. Photos are handled only on the device and are not sent
- **Notifications**: used to show practice reminders. These are local notifications scheduled on the device, not delivered from a server
- **Text-to-speech**: questions and passages are read aloud using the device's built-in speech feature, with no network communication

## Section 3 Purposes of Use

The Provider uses the information it collects for the following purposes.

1. Providing, maintaining, protecting, and improving the App
2. Transcribing recordings, scoring them, and providing advice
3. Improving the App based on analysis of how it is used
4. Providing and managing subscriptions
5. Delivering and measuring ads
6. Responding to inquiries from Users
7. Delivering notices about the App
8. Purposes incidental to the purposes above

## Section 4 Provision to Third Parties

The Provider does not provide personal information to third parties without obtaining the User's prior consent, except in the following cases.

1. Where required by law
2. Where it is necessary to protect the life, body, or property of a person and it is difficult to obtain the User's consent
3. Where it is particularly necessary to improve public health or promote the sound growth of children, and it is difficult to obtain the User's consent
4. Where it is necessary to cooperate with a national agency, a local government, or a party entrusted by either, in carrying out duties prescribed by law, and obtaining the User's consent would be likely to impede the performance of those duties
5. Where provision accompanies the outsourcing or use of external services set out in Section 5

## Section 6 Recorded Audio and the Use of AI

1. Audio recorded in speaking practice and its transcript are sent to Google's Gemini (Firebase AI Logic) in order to generate transcription, scoring, and advice. In mock interviews, the industry and job the User enters, and the transcripts of the interview questions and answers, are also sent. To generate outline hints for a topic, the topic text is sent.
2. Scripts and photos loaded for script reading are not sent.
3. No identifying information such as a name or email address is attached when data is sent.
4. Before recording, the App shows a consent screen explaining the destination, purpose, retention period, whether the data is used for AI training, and how to withdraw, and asks for the User's consent. If the User does not consent, the recording feature is unavailable. Consent can be withdrawn at any time in the App's Settings.
5. The App uses the Gemini API under a paid plan, and the audio sent is not used by Google to train AI models.
6. Users should take care not to include personal information or confidential information, of their own or of third parties, in a recording.
7. Apart from the features described above, the App does not send User input to any external AI service. The App also does not send what the User said, recordings, transcripts, or scripts to usage analytics (Firebase Analytics).
8. Scores and advice are an AI-generated rough guide, and their accuracy is not guaranteed.

## Section 7 Advertising

1. For Users on the free tier, the App may show rewarded ads (video ads) that the User themselves chooses to start. The App does not show banner ads, interstitial ads, or app open ads. Users with a Premium Plan are not shown ads.
2. Ads are delivered using Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. AdMob may collect information such as advertising identifiers for ad delivery and measurement. AdMob's privacy policy is available at [https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
3. For Users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, consent is obtained through a consent management platform (Google UMP) before ads are loaded. If consent is not given, non-personalized ads are delivered.
4. On iOS, the App requests permission through App Tracking Transparency before any tracking using the advertising identifier (IDFA). If the User does not grant permission, no tracking using the IDFA is performed and non-personalized ads are delivered. This permission can be changed at any time in the iOS **Settings** app > **Privacy & Security** > **Tracking**.

## Section 8 Retention Period

1. Recordings stored on the device are kept for 30 days by default, and you can choose 7, 30, or 90 days in Settings. After that period only the recording is deleted; scores, feedback, and transcripts remain on the device. However, for the "you then, and you now" before-and-after listening feature, your first recording is excluded from the retention period and remains on the device (it is deleted if you delete that recording individually or delete all data).
2. You can delete all practice records, recordings, and loaded scripts on the device at once from **Settings** > **Delete all data**. Deleting the App also removes the data on the device.
3. The Provider does not store the audio and transcripts sent to the external AI service. How they are handled on the external service's side follows that service provider's policy.
4. The Provider retains any other personal information only for the period necessary to achieve the purposes of use.

## Section 9 Security Measures

The Provider takes necessary and appropriate measures, such as encrypting communications and restricting access permissions, to prevent leakage, loss, or damage of personal information and otherwise to manage it securely.

## Section 10 Users' Rights

1. A User may request disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, suspension of use, or suspension of provision to third parties, of their own personal information held by the Provider.
2. Requests under the preceding item should be sent to the contact point in Section 13. The Provider will verify the requester's identity and respond in accordance with applicable law.
3. Data on the device can also be deleted from the App's Settings.

## Section 11 Use by Minors

A minor who uses the App must obtain the prior consent of a parent or other legal representative.

2. Where a change is material, the Provider will announce the content of the change and the date it takes effect, by display within the App or by other appropriate means.
