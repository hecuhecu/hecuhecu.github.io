## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a subscription to start practicing?

No. The daily topic on the Home screen (one per day), the score on the result screen, and the script-reading warm-up are free to use.

Premium (subscription) adds the six-category breakdown, overall comment, model rewrite and transcript on the result screen, all seven kinds of practice in the Practice tab, the second and later daily topics each day, and the Records tab (trend, calendar, and list). The annual plan includes a 3-day free trial.

### Where are my recordings sent?

For scoring, transcription, and advice, the recorded audio and its transcript are sent to Google's Gemini (Firebase AI Logic). In mock interviews, the industry and job you type are sent as well. Scripts and photos you load for script reading are never sent. A consent screen is shown before recording, and if you do not consent, recording is unavailable.

Your audio is not used to train AI. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app's Settings. See the [Privacy Policy](/apps/talkerz/en/privacy/) for details.

### When are my recordings deleted?

Recordings are kept on your device for 30 days by default. You can choose 7, 30, or 90 days in Settings. After that period only the recording is deleted; your scores, feedback, and transcripts remain.

### I see a message that I have reached today's limit

Practices that use AI have a daily limit, which resets when the date changes. After you reach the limit, practices that do not use AI, such as script reading, remain available.

### I want to see the full evaluation without subscribing

On the result screen, you can watch a rewarded ad to the end to see the full evaluation for that session. With Premium there are no ads.

### How do I cancel the free trial?

You can cancel in the iOS **Settings** app > your name > **Subscriptions**. **If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the free period ends, the annual plan price is charged when that period ends.**

After cancelling, Premium features remain available until the free period ends.

### My records disappeared after I changed devices

Talkerz has no accounts, and your practice records, recordings, and scripts are stored only on your device, so they are not carried over to a new device.

If you have already purchased, you can restore your subscription status with "Restore Purchases" on the purchase screen, using the same Apple ID you purchased with.

### I disagree with how my speech was scored

Scores are an AI-generated rough guide, not a definitive judgment of how well you speak. Please use them to see trends. If you have doubts, contact the email address above; we use what you send us to improve the App.

### I would like a refund

Refunds follow Apple's policy, and we cannot issue refunds directly. Please request a refund through Apple's support page ([https://reportaproblem.apple.com/](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/)).

### I want to delete my data

In the app, go to **Settings** > **Delete all data** to delete all practice records, recordings, and loaded scripts. Deleted data cannot be restored. Deleting the App also removes the data on your device.

If you have an active paid plan, you must cancel the subscription separately from deleting the data.
