## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. What does this app do?**
A. It lets you check and remove the metadata contained in your photos, such as location (GPS), camera model, and date and time taken, in one tap. Before you share a photo on social media or in a messaging app, you can safely erase the privacy information you can't see.

**Q. Are my photos sent to an external server?**
A. No. Selecting photos, analyzing metadata, removing it, and saving the result all happen entirely on your device. Your photos are never sent to our servers.

**Q. Where are cleaned photos saved?**
A. When you choose **Save**, a new copy with the metadata removed is added to your photo library. The original photo is not modified.

**Q. Which photo formats are supported?**
A. JPEG, HEIC, and PNG.

**Q. What metadata is removed?**
A. In addition to location (GPS coordinates), camera model, and date and time taken, the App removes Exif/TIFF/IPTC and other metadata, MakerNote, GPS information inside embedded thumbnails, and GPS-related information in XMP. The image itself (pixel data) and its orientation are not changed.

**Q. How can I tell whether the metadata was removed?**
A. After processing, the App automatically verifies the result. If no metadata remains, a green check mark is shown. If any remaining metadata is detected, a red × is shown and the Save button is disabled.

**Q. What can I do for free?**
A. You can select photos one at a time and check, remove, save, and share their metadata. The free version shows ads.

**Q. What does Pro (paid) add?**
A. You can process multiple photos at once in a batch, and choose individually which items to remove (location / date and time / camera model). Ads are also removed. Pro is available as a subscription and/or a one-time purchase; the options and prices are shown in the App and on the App Store.

**Q. Do I need to buy again if I change devices?**
A. If you use the same Apple ID, you can restore your purchase with **Restore Purchases** on the purchase screen.

**Q. How do I cancel a subscription?**
A. In the iOS **Settings** app > your name > **Subscriptions**, select this App and tap **Cancel Subscription**. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. After cancelling, Pro features remain available until the end of the billing period.

**Q. I would like a refund.**
A. Refunds are handled by Apple, and we cannot issue refunds directly. Please request a refund through Apple's support page ([https://reportaproblem.apple.com/](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/)).

**Q. What happens if I select a Live Photo?**
A. Only the still image is cleaned of metadata. The video part is not processed and may still contain location data; the App lets you know about this when processing.

## How to Restore Purchases

1. Open the App
2. Go to **Settings** > **Subscription**
3. Tap **Restore Purchases** on the purchase screen
