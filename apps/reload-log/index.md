## Your handloading data, all in one ledger

Reload Log is a dedicated ledger for handloaders and precision-rifle shooters. Link your load recipes to real range results and track every component lot for true, repeatable load development.

## Key Features

- **Recipe ↔ Results**: Tie each load recipe (powder charge, bullet, primer, brass, COAL) to grouping/MOA and chronograph velocity (ES/SD) in one record.
- **Range Sessions**: Log group sizes, velocities, and ES/SD, with photos of your targets.
- **Component Lot Tracking**: Track powder, primer, and bullet lots so you know exactly which lot produced which result.
- **Inventory & Cost**: Keep component inventory and calculate per-round cost.
- **Charge-Range Alert**: Compare a load against a maximum value you enter from published manufacturer data. The app never recommends charge weights.
- **Node Visualization (Premium)**: See a charge vs. velocity vs. group-size chart to find your load's node.
- **PDF Data Sheets**: Export a clean data sheet for any recipe.

## Privacy

All of your records and photos are stored only on your device. Reload Log uses no advertising and no analytics, and never sends your data to a server.
