# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

hecuhecu ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the mobile application "Reload Log" (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect personally identifiable information.

All data you enter — including load recipes (powder charge, bullet, primer, brass, COAL, etc.), range session results (grouping/MOA, chronograph velocity, ES/SD), component lot records, inventory, and cost calculations — is stored **only on your device** in a local database. We never transmit or store this data on our servers or any external service.

### Camera and Photo Library

The App requests access to your camera and photo library so you can attach photos (such as target groups or component lot labels) to your records. Photos you capture or select are stored **only on your device** for display within the App and are never transmitted externally.

## 2. How We Use Information

Any information handled by the App is used solely for the following purposes:

- Providing the App's features
- Improving quality and fixing bugs
- Preventing fraudulent use
- Processing purchases (where applicable)

## 3. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not disclose information to third parties except as required by law.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service for purchase processing. Please review its privacy policy.

The App uses "RevenueCat," provided by RevenueCat, Inc., to process purchases. To handle purchase receipts obtained from the App Store, the following information is sent to RevenueCat's servers:

- An anonymous user ID (which does not identify you personally)
- Purchase receipt information
- Technical information such as app version and OS version

RevenueCat's Privacy Policy:
[https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)

The App uses Apple (App Store / StoreKit) to process payments.
[https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

The App does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

## 5. Safety Feature: Charge-Range Alerts

The App can compare a load you record against a maximum charge value **that you enter yourself** from published manufacturer data, and alert you when your recorded charge exceeds it. This comparison happens entirely on your device. The App does not recommend, prescribe, or suggest charge weights, and it does not provide load data of its own — it is a record-keeping and data-management tool only.

## 6. Where Data Is Stored

All user data created or generated by the App is stored only on your device. Records, photos, and calculations are never sent to a server.

## 7. Your Rights

You can stop all use of your data at any time by uninstalling the App. The App also provides a way to delete in-app data from within its settings screen.
