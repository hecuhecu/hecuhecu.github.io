## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Where are my load recipes, sessions, and lots stored?**
A. Everything is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. If you delete the App, your data is deleted with it, so please export a backup first if you want to keep it.

**Q. What is the camera / photo access used for?**
A. Only to attach photos — such as target groups or component lot labels — to your records. Photos stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

**Q. What does the charge-range alert do? Does the app tell me how much powder to load?**
A. No. Reload Log never recommends or suggests a charge weight and provides no load data of its own. It only compares a load against a maximum value **that you enter yourself** from published manufacturer data, and warns you if your recorded charge exceeds it. It is a record-keeping tool, not a load manual. Always follow current published data from your component manufacturers.

**Q. What's included for free?**
A. Load recipe management, range session records (grouping/MOA, chronograph velocity, ES/SD), component lot tracking, inventory, and cost calculation are available for free. The node visualization (charge vs. velocity vs. group-size chart) and unlimited recipes/sessions are unlocked with Premium.

**Q. What are the Premium options?**
A. A one-time Lifetime purchase ($49.99), and auto-renewing subscriptions (Monthly $2.99 / Yearly $18.99). The Yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial.

**Q. I changed devices and my purchase is gone.**
A. Use "Restore Purchases" in the settings screen to restore purchases made with the same Apple ID. Because records are stored on-device, we recommend exporting a backup before switching devices.

## How to Cancel a Subscription

1. Open the iOS **Settings** app
2. Tap your Apple ID at the top
3. Tap **Subscriptions**
4. Select **Reload Log**
5. Tap **Cancel Subscription**

You can cancel during the free trial the same way. If you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, you will not be charged.

## Deleting Your Data

You can delete in-app data from the App's "Settings" → "Delete Data". Uninstalling the App also removes all on-device data.
